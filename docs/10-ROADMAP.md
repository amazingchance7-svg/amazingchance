# Roadmap

## Sprint 0 — Foundation
AI guide, product and architecture documentation, Cursor rules, local environment, Prisma schema, migrations, and database health.

## Sprint 1 — Core infrastructure
Prisma module, Prisma service, database-aware health endpoint, configuration validation, error handling, and correlation IDs.

## Sprint 2 — Draws
Draw lifecycle, weekly scheduling, sales windows, and transition tests.

## Sprint 3 — Purchases and tickets
Purchase creation, idempotency, pricing, ticket issuance, and ticket listing.

## Sprint 4 — Payments
Provider abstraction, checkout, signed webhooks, duplicate handling, and refunds.

## Sprint 5 — Draw integrity
Sales closure, canonical snapshot, SHA-256 hash, finalization, and public verification data.

## Sprint 6 — RANDOM.ORG
Provider adapter, evidence, verification, and manual-review paths.

## Sprint 7 — Winners and prizes
Deterministic selection, 50/30/20 distribution, prize records, and publication.

## Sprint 8 — Ledger and audit
Double-entry ledger, 70/20/10 allocation, prize liability, refunds, and audit log.
